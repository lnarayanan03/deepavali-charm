A glowing Deepavali star lantern for the GNOME top bar.

- Drag the star and let go to throw it
- It swings when you switch workspaces or open the overview
- Click it for a shower of marigold, rose and jasmine petals
- Drag the little gold hook to move where it hangs

Install: link this folder to ~/.local/share/gnome-shell/extensions/deepavali-charm@ranga, log out and back in, then `gnome-extensions enable deepavali-charm@ranga`.
Test without touching your desktop: `./test.sh /tmp/out` (headless GNOME Shell, saves screenshots).
